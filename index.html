About

I'm a full-stack engineer at Deque Systems, the accessibility company behind axe. I build features for Deque's accessibility products with TypeScript and Node.js, from the interface down to the services and infrastructure that run them.

Before Deque I spent five years building product frontends and the systems behind them. At CAW Studios I led teams on client products, designed the monorepo behind Hoichoi's web and TV apps and cut their page load times in half. At Instill I grew from frontend into full-stack work on an AI-driven workplace platform. At HighLevel I built Vue micro frontends and NestJS APIs for a CRM used at very large scale.

I care about software that is fast, accessible and easy to run. I learn new stacks quickly, take features from design to production, and use AI coding tools to automate the slow parts of development. I studied Electrical Engineering at IIT (ISM) Dhanbad.
